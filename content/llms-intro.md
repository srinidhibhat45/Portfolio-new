# Srinidhi Bhat

> Srinidhi Bhat (Shri) is a design engineer based in Goa, India. He designs maritime software at OneOcean and takes freelance work in UI/UX and product design, brand identity, posters and print, presentations and mock-ups. This site is his portfolio: {{count}} case studies, the services he offers, his experience and how to reach him.

Key facts:

- Name: Srinidhi Bhat. Goes by Shri. Writes poems, essays and fiction as अvinash, in English, Hindi and Konkani.
- Role: design engineer. He designs digital products and also builds them.
- Location: Goa, India.
- Current work: UX Designer at OneOcean since 2025, designing maritime products and contributing to the Caspian Design System, across research, interfaces and prototypes.
- Experience: 4+ years designing, 30+ projects and concepts.
- Availability: open for freelance project work and ongoing collaborations.

Services:

- UI/UX design: web and mobile products.
- Brand and logo: visual identity systems.
- Posters and print: campaigns, events and print.
- Presentations: pitch decks and company presentations.
- Mock-ups: launch visuals and product previews.

Experience:

- 2025 to now: UX Designer, OneOcean. Designing maritime products and contributing to the Caspian Design System.
- 2024 to 2025: UI/UX Designer, Genora Infotech. Led UI/UX design for Genora and Goan Narrative, from early concepts to developer handoff.
- 2022: Software Intern, Persistent Systems. Built chatbots with Python, Java and Azure pipelines; trained in QA automation.
- 2021 to 2024: UI/UX Designer and Outreach Lead, Linktopus. Designed products and led outreach, contributing to a 25%+ increase in engagement and sales.
