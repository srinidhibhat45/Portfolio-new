How it is put together

Two ways into the same match, built differently

Solo

One player's browser

One player at the keyboard.

↓

AI fill

Computer-run crew fill the empty seats.

↓

Overview pauses

Opening the full overview pauses the match.

The same arena

3 v 3, eight relays, 10 minutes at most, keyboard and mouse.

Room

A host's browser

The match depends on the host keeping the page open.

↓

Direct peer connections

Players join through direct peer connections. There is no dedicated match server.

↓

No host migration

If the host leaves, the match cannot move to another player. The overview does not pause.
