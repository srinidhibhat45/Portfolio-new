The build

What happens to a post between the file and the page

In

A Markdown file

One folder per post, with its images beside it. Written over five years with no house rules.

→
Step 1

Verse

A fence with no language becomes a poem with its line breaks and stanza gaps.

→
Step 2

Heading levels

The F1 primer opens at level 3, one Murder part at level 5. Each post now starts at level 2, in the same order, at the same size.

→
Step 3

Figures

A paragraph of only images becomes a figure, or a side-by-side group. Long alt text becomes a caption.

→
Out

A static page

Plus search-engine data from the same breadcrumb list, a sitemap entry with the post's own date, and a search index.

Steps 1 to 3 are small plugins made for this site. Search is built by Pagefind after the pages exist.
