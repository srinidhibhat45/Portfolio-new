How a post joins a series

The title decides the series and the order

Post titles, as written

The Murder in Classroom-10 (Part 1)
The Murder in Classroom-10 (Part 2)
The Murder in Classroom 10 (Part 3)

Parts 1 and 2 spell the name with a hyphen. Part 3 does not. Only the ending is matched, so all three still join.

→

The series rule

Ends with (Part N)
N sets the order. The matched words are taken off the title when it is listed.

The other two series use (Shri's Podcast: Ep #N) and All About F1 – #N.

→

What a reader gets

The Murder in Classroom 10

  1. Part 1
  2. Part 2
  3. Part 3

Rules are edited in the editor's settings. A rule that is not valid is skipped with a warning, so a typo cannot stop the site from building.
