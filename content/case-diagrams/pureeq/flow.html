How a new profile is made

Eight steps, about five to six minutes

Set up: make the answers trustworthy

1

Headphones

In-ear or over-ear, guessed from the name. Optionally the model, from over 6,000 measured ones.

2

Room check

Optional. The mic listens for 3 seconds to judge background noise. Nothing is stored.

3

Mode

What the headphones are mostly for: Auto, Gaming, Music, Voice or General.

since 1.7
4

Volume

Set a repeating beep to “soft but clear”. The check pauses if the volume moves.

5

Left and right

One beep on one side. Catches swapped earbuds and mono audio.

↓

Two questions

6

Listening check

Did you hear that beep? 7 pitches per ear, 500 Hz to 8 kHz. Quick about 1½ min, Standard about 2, Thorough about 3.

7

Taste test

A or B? Blind pairs on the built-in track or your own song. Usually 5 to 7 rounds.

→

The result

8

Your sound

Hear it with EQ on and off at the same loudness, fine-tune, save. Then apply it to all audio or export it.

One profile belongs to one pair of headphones and one listener. In version 1.1 a whole profile took 13 to 15 minutes; the faster check in 1.2 brought it to 5 to 6.
