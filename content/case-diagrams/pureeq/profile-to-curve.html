→

Worked out from it, for each ear

1

Headphone correction

The model's lab measurement brought to the Harman target, a curve most listeners prefer. Boosts at most +6 dB, half strength in the high treble.

2

Ear tuning

The smooth trend of the check, with 500 Hz to 1.5 kHz as the zero line. Small gaps get the chosen strength, large ones less. Both ears share broad filters; a real left/right difference is added on top.

3

Taste

A bass shelf at 105 Hz and a broad treble shelf from 2.5 kHz, set by the taste test.

4

Mode and volume

The mode's own filters. On the phone-wide EQ, bass is added back as you turn down below the tuned volume.

5

Bass guard and preamp

Limits on deep bass, then an overall level set for real music, so the EQ does not clip.

All audio on the phone
