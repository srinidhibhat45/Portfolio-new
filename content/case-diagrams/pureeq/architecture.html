How it is put together

Everything runs on the phone, around one profile

Inside the app

Screens

Jetpack Compose: setup, check, taste, fine-tune, export, settings.

↓

Test sessions

The listening check and taste test, each with its own model that picks the next question.

↓

Audio engine

Plays beeps and A/B music on its own sound thread, and stops 3 seconds after the last sound.

→

The core

Profile

Answers and choices only, saved on the phone.

↓

Curve maker

Works out every curve and filter from the profile: correction, ear tuning, taste, mode, bass guard, preamp.

↓

Exporters

Eight kinds of output, from slider values to impulse-response files for convolver apps.

→

Out to Android

Background service

Holds Android's equaliser effect on the output mix, one switch per output. Shows a notification.

Quick Settings tile

On, off, profile and Fine-tune from the swipe-down panel.

Only two trips online

AutoEq's public headphone data when you look up a model, and the GitHub build's check for its own updates.

No account, no analytics, no server of my own. Kotlin and AndroidX only, with no other libraries at run time.
