Fine-tune: hear it, change it, put it back

123456
1EQ on and Off are matched in loudness, so you hear a change in balance, not a jump in volume.
2Compare on the built-in track or on a song of your own.
3Reset puts every slider back to what the tests suggested, with Undo. It is dim here because nothing has changed yet.
4Add the measured correction for your headphone model at any time, without redoing the tests.
5Ear tuning sets how much of the listening check to apply. 75% is the default.
6Each slider says what it changes in plain words, such as “kick drum and bass guitar weight”, and shows its value in dB.

From the Play Store screenshots made for version 1.6. A moved slider also shows the suggested value under it; tap it to put back just that slider.
