Where the taste test starts

Most listeners like the same balance. The test starts there.

Three groups of listeners in a published Harman study (Olive, Welti and Khonsaripour, 2019)

Like the target as it isno change64%
Want more bassabout +4.5 dB15%
Want less bass, a bit more trebleabout −2.5 dB bass, +1.5 dB treble21%

Then each round

1Two complete settings play on the same music, blind, at the same loudness.
2You pick A, B or Same. Answers wait a second after a new pair, and until you have heard both.
3The app updates its guess of your ideal bass and treble, then picks the pair expected to leave the least error.
4It stops once the guess settles, after 4 to 8 rounds. Usually 5 to 7.

The starting guess is widened by half when no headphone model is picked, because the headphones may then be far from the target.
