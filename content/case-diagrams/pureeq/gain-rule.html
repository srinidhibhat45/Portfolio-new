Ear tuning, the rule since version 1.5

Small gaps get most of a lift. Large gaps get a gentler one.

Up to 6 dB

0.75 dB of boost for each dB of gap. Small, smooth gaps are mostly the headphones' own sound.

Beyond 6 dB

About 0.35 dB per dB. A full lift sounds harsh. A published fitting rule, NAL-R, uses 0.31, and people tuning for themselves chose about 0.29 (Sabin et al. 2020).

Bands heard better

Cut, but at most 6 dB. Since 1.7.3 a cut both ears share counts half.

Drawn from the app's fitting rule for the 500 Hz to 4 kHz bands. It shows the rule, not a measured result. Below 400 Hz and above 8 kHz the tuning fades out.
