Shritija, 19, a medical student

Shritija is not a real person. She stands for one of the three groups we named: students and teenagers.

About her

  • Lives with stress and anxiety attacks
  • Introverted

What keeps her going

  • Talking to her best friend
  • Reading books
  • Quiet green spaces, now and then

What she wants

  • To approach a genuine therapist
  • To get back to a calmer state of mind
  • To focus on her studies without distractions

What gets in the way

  • Her family does not support her seeing a therapist
  • Her course leaves little time for something like therapy
  • She cannot handle her problems alone and needs help soon
