How the scale switch behaves

Seven categories follow the switch. Four have a reason to ignore it.

Follow the switch

True scale by default. Log scale squashes the gap so both sides can be seen.

MoneyHeightDistanceLandWeightTimeVolume
  • Weight's beam always tips by the log of the ratio. Only the blocks on the pans change.

Not a size

Their picture does not grow, so there is nothing to squash.

TemperatureSpeed
  • Temperature is a position on one shared scale.
  • Speed is two cars on the same 400 m lap. The gap shows as time.

A count or a ladder

Their picture is already built to read.

DataEnergy
  • Data draws one square per unit, the same size on both sides.
  • Energy is always a log ladder. Each line is ten times the one below.

At true scale

Nothing drops to zero. The smaller side keeps a minimum of 2 to 6 units on a drawing 1,000 units wide, and a note says the speck is the honest answer.

At log scale

Anything that would now be false is removed: the height ruler, and the person standing beside the cash and the cubes.

Drawn from the app's own drawing rules for each category.
