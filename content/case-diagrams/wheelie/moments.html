How it works

Three moments of a group ride, three tabs

Before: stopped

Map tab

Form the group

Create a group, share a 6-letter code or a link, see who is online.

Pick where to go

Search a place. The app shares it with the group and works out a meeting point.

Check the route

Travel time first, then "meets your group on the way", then one big Start.

→
During: moving

Map and Voice tabs

Follow the turns

A large next-turn banner, arrival time, distance to go and speed.

See the others

Each rider's position, updated every 2 seconds at most.

Talk

Push to talk or open mic through your own helmet headset. Voice keeps running while you are on the map.

→
After: arrived

Rides tab

Keep the ride

Every finished ride becomes a card: route shape, distance, time, average and top speed.

Share it

The card is saved as an image and sent through the phone's share sheet.

Nothing saved if you stop early

A ride ended before arriving is not kept.

The map tab is never closed. The other tabs sit beside it, so the map and voice keep their state when you switch.
