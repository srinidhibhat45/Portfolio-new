A decision checked on a real route

A car route is not a motorcycle route

Car profile: over the Sea LinkMotorcycle costing: around it

What was wrong

Routing used a car profile. It would send a motorcycle over bridges and expressways where two-wheelers are banned.

What I ruled out

The first router has only car, bicycle and walking profiles. A bicycle profile is wrong the other way: it avoids highways motorcycles may use.

What I changed

A second free router, Valhalla, with its motorcycle setting. It reads OpenStreetMap's two-wheeler access tags. The first router stays as a fallback.

The limit

It is only as good as OpenStreetMap's tags. A ban nobody has tagged can still slip through.
