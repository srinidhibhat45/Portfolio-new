How the meeting point is chosen

First look for a road everyone already takes. Only then compromise.

1

Route every rider to the destination

Each from where they are now. It needs at least two known positions.

Fewer than 2 riders: no meeting point
2

Look for where the routes merge

Walk back from the destination along the longest route. Find the earliest point after which every other route stays on the same road.

"Same road" means within 60 m, checked every 50 m
3

Keep it only if it is worth stopping for

"Meet 400 m before the café" is just the café.

At least 500 m before the destination, or 5% of the longest route if that is more
4

No merge? Try the places routes pass closest

Score each by the extra distance for the rider it costs most. Pick the lowest.

Rejected if it adds more than 15% to anyone's ride
5

Nothing passes: no meeting point

Everyone rides straight to the destination. A forced detour is worse than no meeting point.

Routing fails: the same. The ride still works.

A merge point from step 2 adds no distance for anyone, so it needs no detour cap.
