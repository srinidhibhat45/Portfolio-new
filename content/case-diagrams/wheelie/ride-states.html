Off the route

More than 50 m off the line for 3 location updates in a row, and the app fetches a new route. A small "Re-routing…" chip shows while it does.

No meeting point

If none is worth it, the ride goes straight from Preview to the destination in one leg.

Ended early

End at any point returns to Idle. A ride that never arrived is not saved.

Why the pause: nobody gets routed on toward the destination alone while the group is still gathering.
