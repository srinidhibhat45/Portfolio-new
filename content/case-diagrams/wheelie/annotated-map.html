The map is the home screen, and every control is thumb-sized

1234567
1"Where to?" is a 64px pill. Search opens full screen with 72px result rows.
2Ride settings. This is where simulated location is switched on for demos.
3Other riders: a colour, a heading arrow and a name. Colour is never the only clue, and an offline rider gets a different icon.
4The group button. A count badge shows how many riders are online.
5Zoom in and out are 64px buttons, so nobody has to pinch with gloves on.
6Follow me. Its on state is shown by fill and by its spoken name, "Following you".
7The OpenStreetMap credit stays visible on every map state.
