How group voice travels

Each phone talks straight to every other phone

Audio: phone to phone

WebRTC, the same standard browsers use for calls. Mono speech with a 24 kbps target, to go easy on mobile data.

Finding each other

Phones swap connection details over a broadcast channel on the group's backend. No extra tables are needed.

When a network blocks it

A free public server finds each phone's address. A free relay carries the audio when a direct link fails.

Why not Bluetooth between bikes

Phone-to-phone Bluetooth audio is not possible with standard phone software. Each rider pairs their own headset to their own phone.

The voice room is the ride group: there is nothing separate to pair. Live audio between two real devices has not been tested yet.
