How it is put together

Every service is free, and each sits behind a small swappable part

On the phone

Flutter app

One codebase for Android and the web. Map, Voice and Rides tabs.

Ride, group and voice state

Held in Riverpod. The screens only read it.

Ride history

Saved on the phone. Nothing about past rides leaves it unless you share a card.

Stand-ins

Without backend keys, a pretend group, pretend voice and simulated GPS take over.

↔

Free public services

Map tiles

OpenStreetMap, credited on screen.

Search

Photon, with Nominatim as fallback.

Routing

Valhalla with motorcycle costing. OSRM as fallback.

↔

Supabase, free tier

Sign-in

Email and password, one stable id per rider.

Groups and members

Postgres tables with access rules in the database. Only members can read a group.

Live positions

Sent as presence messages and never written to the database. No location history is kept.

Voice setup

A broadcast channel for phones to find each other.

↔

Between riders

WebRTC audio

Phone to phone, planned for up to 6 riders.

STUN and TURN

Free public servers to connect phones across networks.

Anything that talks to the network lives behind one small class, so a free server can be swapped for a self-hosted one without touching the screens.
