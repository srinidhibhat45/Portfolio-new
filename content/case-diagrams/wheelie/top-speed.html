A bug the share card made obvious

One bad location fix put 4,475 km/h on a ride card

Top speed, before the fix4,475 km/h
Top speed, after the fix103.3 km/h

The cause

Top speed was the fastest gap between two location fixes. One jump, from a bad fix or the demo's fast-forward, became the headline number.

The rule now

Any stretch faster than 299 km/h is left out of top speed. Distance and average speed still count every stretch, because one spike barely moves them.

Both numbers come from simulated rides in the app, before and after the change, as I recorded them at the time. Neither is a real ride.
