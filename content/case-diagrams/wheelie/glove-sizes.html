The glove rules, to scale

Big, forgiving targets were set as rules before any screen

24pxThe WCAG 2.2 AA minimum target
48pxWheelie's floor for anything you can tap
64pxPrimary actions: start, end, zoom, talk modes
72pxEach row of search results
88pxHeight of the bottom tab bar
128pxThe hold-to-talk button

8px apart

The minimum gap between two targets, so a glove does not hit both.

Tap or a straight drag

No pinch, no multi-finger gestures, no long press as the only way to do something.

Never colour alone

Online, offline, speaking and errors always have an icon or words too.

Feel every tap

Each glove button gives a vibration, because a gloved finger cannot feel a light tap land.

Squares drawn at twice their size, in proportion to each other. These are the targets the app is built to. They are not a measured result.
