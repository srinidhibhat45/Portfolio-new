The problem in one picture

Three riders, three towns, one destination. Where do they meet?

Riders start from different places. Before they can ride together, they need a spot on the way where everyone can meet.

What Wheelie answers

Each rider's own fastest route to Mapusa already passes the Mandovi crossing. Meeting there adds no distance for anyone.

The obvious guess was wrong

The obvious guess was a well-known junction on the way. One rider's fastest road never touches it, so the true merge point is the bridge.

Schematic, not to scale. The towns are the app's built-in demo scenario. The lines show the shape of the routes, not the real roads.
