The problem in one picture

The race data is free, but it sits in three places and each one has a gap

OpenF1, a public data service

Has: timing, GPS positions, car telemetry, laps, tyres, pit stops, race control, team radio, weather.

Gap: a free user may make about 3 requests a second, and is locked out while a session is live.

The official F1 live-timing feed

Has: timing, gaps, sectors, tyres, flags, race control and radio, during the session.

Gap: no GPS and no car telemetry for free, and a web page cannot open it directly.

Jolpica, a public results archive

Has: championship standings and season results.

Gap: nothing about the race in progress.
