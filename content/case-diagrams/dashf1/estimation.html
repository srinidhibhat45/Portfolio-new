Placing a car without GPS

Each car is placed between the last timing line it crossed and the next

1Learn the lines

Every crossing tells the model where a line really is. Averaged over the field, the layout settles on the truth.

2Move at own pace

Between lines, a car moves at its measured pace and is held just short of the line it has not reached.

3Check the gap

A car 2 seconds back sits where the leader was 2 seconds ago. That nudges the estimate, and takes over if crossings go stale.

4Smooth the dot

The dot is steered toward the estimate, always forward, closing any error in about a second.

An illustration of the method, not real race data. The lap is drawn straight here; on screen it follows the circuit outline.
