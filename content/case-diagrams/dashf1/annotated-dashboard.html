A closer look

One screen, seven parts

1234567
1Pick any season, Grand Prix and session. Weather and track status sit on the right.
2The timing tower: order, interval, gap, tyre and its age, last and best lap. Click a driver to follow them.
3The track map, rebuilt from GPS, with every car in its team colour.
4Nine tabs below the map: Predict, Lap Times, Positions, Strategy, All Cars, Pit Stops, Weather, Stats, Standings.
5Telemetry for the followed car, or the leader if you have not picked one.
6Race control messages and team radio, kept in step with the replay.
7Play, scrub, and speed up from 1× to 60×.
