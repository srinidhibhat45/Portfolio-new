A decision I reversed

From estimated numbers for everything to estimates only where they are labelled

22 July 2026

Live mode filled every panel

  • Speed, gear, rpm, throttle and brake for every car, worked out from a per-circuit profile and the driver's pace.
  • Each value carried a small "(est)" tag.
  • Cars moved on the map four times a second by dead reckoning, a guess from pace between timing points.
→

26 July 2026

Race mode leaves those panels out

  • No dashes or invented numbers stand in for channels the free feed does not carry.
  • One thing is still estimated, the car's place on track, from a new model that learns from the timing lines.
  • It is labelled in three places: under the map, on the driver card and in the bottom bar.

Both versions are in the project history. Full telemetry stays available in replay after the session.
