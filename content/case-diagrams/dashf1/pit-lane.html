Before and after, one race condition

Cars in the pit lane used to freeze mid-circuit

Before

A pitting car stopped at the last timing line it crossed. Under a safety car, when many cars pit together, half the field could sit still on the circuit.

After

It is walked from where it left the racing line to the pit exit, over the time cars are seen to spend in the lane, drawn just off the racing line.

An illustration of the change described in the project history, not a capture of a real race.
