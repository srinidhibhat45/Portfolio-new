How a song is stored

One set of chords, three ways to play it

Level 1

Just the chords

What you play: block chords, one per bar. The left hand is optional.

Who it is for: day one. Sing over it.

●○○

Level 2

Simple melody

What you play: the chords plus the notes the tune lands on.

Who it is for: it already sounds like the song. Good for background music.

●●○

Level 3

Full melody

What you play: the real melody with proper chord shapes.

Who it is for: the version you are working towards.

●●●

All three levels share one set of harmony: song → sections (verse, chorus) → bars, each with a short lyric cue → the notes to press

The level names are the ones in the app. Each level carries a difficulty from one to three, drawn here as dots.
