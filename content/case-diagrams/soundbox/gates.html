Room noise

The first second measures the room with nobody playing. That much is taken off every reading after.

Loud enough

Two thresholds, one to switch on and a lower one to switch off, so a ringing chord does not flicker.

3

Shaped like music

Music puts its energy in three or four of the twelve note names. A fan or traffic spreads it flat.

→
4

A hand's worth

A hand plays three or four notes, never eight. A reading that broad is refused, not trimmed.

Did it come from a piano, and which notes?

5

Piano gate

Checks the shape of the sound over time: a struck string arrives at once, only gets quieter and holds its pitch. A voice does none of those.

Main detector: Basic Pitch

A small open-source note-transcription model, run on the phone. It hears each key with its octave. Needs WebGL.

→

Fallback

A chromagram for chords and autocorrelation for single notes, when the model cannot run.
