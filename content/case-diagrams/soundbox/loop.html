The practice loop in Wait for me

Nothing moves until you play the right thing

SeeThe current chord is large, the next one small, and its keys are lit
→
PlayOn a real piano, or a digital one plugged in by USB
→
ListenThe microphone, or MIDI, reports what is sounding
→
JudgeDoes it match the step you are on?

It matches

The song moves to the next step. Your furthest point is saved.

A wrong note

Shown in a neutral amber. The song simply waits. No red cross, no buzzer.

It is not sure

It says “listening…” instead of guessing. If it heard singing, it says “that sounds sung, not played”.

Play along adds a metronome and Hear it plays the song to you. All three share one position in the song, so switching never loses your place.
