How it works

From four answers to a picture you can keep

1Four answers

Name, birth date, birth time and birth place. Time is optional and starts at midnight.

2Find the place

OpenStreetMap's place search turns the typed place into a latitude and a longitude.

3Find the moment

Local time becomes world time (UTC). The offset is guessed from longitude: one hour per 15°.

4Set the sky clock

That moment becomes a day count and a "star time" for the spot on Earth (Julian date and sidereal time).

↓ then

5Place every object

416 stars, 21 nebulae, galaxies and clusters, the Milky Way, the Sun, the Moon and 5 planets get a height and a direction.

6Flatten the dome

A stereographic projection lays the curved sky flat, the way a map flattens the globe.

7Paint in layers

Up to 11 layers on one canvas, chosen by the mode and four switches.

8Keep it

The same painter runs again at 2160×3840 or 3840×2160 and saves a PNG.

Also on every reveal, in the current version: the name, birth date, birth time, place, latitude and longitude are sent to a Google Sheet. The page does not say so today.

Steps 3 to 8 run in the browser in plain JavaScript, with no libraries.
