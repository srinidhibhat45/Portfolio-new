Tried when the app comes to the front and every two minutes while it is in use. A try that takes over 12 seconds stops and waits.

A film and a series can share a number in the database, so every record is "movie:" or "tv:" plus the number.

Deletions leave a marker

A tombstone stops a title I removed from coming back from another device.

Newer wins

Conflicts compare update times, all turned into UTC first.

Never synced

API keys. A failure shows as changes waiting, never as "synced".
