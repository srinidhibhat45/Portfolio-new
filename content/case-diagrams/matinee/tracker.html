The rule I wrote down

A tracker, not a streaming browser

Every feature had to land in one of three places.

Upfront

One tap from the main screens.

  • Log a title as seen, with or without a rating
  • Bookmark it for later
  • Recommendations, each with a reason
  • Bookmarks and Seen in the Library
  • Stats on viewing habits

Behind a menu

Still there, one step further away.

  • Sorting, collections, Watching, Diary and Hidden, in Library tools
  • Episode tracking, rewatches, soundtrack and feedback, under More on a title
  • Pacing, genres and era, under More preferences in the picker

Left out

What streaming apps put first.

  • Provider logos in the feed and on title pages
  • Subscription, rent and buy sections
  • A row of buttons under every poster
  • A second recommendation picker in the feed
