How the app is laid out

Four places, one picker, and a title page under everything

Discover

  • Search first: titles, series, people
  • All, Movies or Series
  • Genre chips
  • Recommended for you
  • Rails: trending, recently watched, popular, top rated

Upcoming

  • Releases in my languages
  • This week or this month
  • New seasons of series
  • Remind me, or add to calendar

Stats

  • Title counts and ratings
  • Watch time
  • Genre and rating charts
  • A calendar heatmap of what I watched each day

Library

  • Two sections: Bookmarks and Seen
  • All, Movies, Series
  • Library tools: sort, collections, Watching, Diary, Hidden
  • Settings and cloud sync

Title page

Opens from any poster. Watched, Watchlist and Rate sit at the top. Everything else is under More.

Pick for tonight

A floating button on every tab. Mood, type and length first, then a short list of three.

Phones get a bottom bar for the four places. Wider windows get a side rail.
