How a feed is built

From my ratings to a feed of 120 titles

1 Profile

Read the library

  • Genres I rate 8 or more gain weight. Genres I rate 5 or less lose more.
  • "Not interested" counts against a genre.
  • Directors and actors are scored the same way.
  • Count how much I watch in each language.
→

2 Candidates

Gather titles

  • 10 seed titles, taken in turn from each language I watch
  • Titles similar to each seed
  • Popular titles in each of my languages
  • Up to 20 titles from each favourite director or actor
  • Trending this week
→

3 Score

Add up eleven terms

  • How strongly my own ratings vouch for it
  • Genre match, language, quality, age
  • Penalties for unreleased titles and people I dislike
  • Keep the strongest reason as the label
→

4 Arrange

Fill the feed

  • Reserve room for each chosen language
  • No more than two from one franchise before a penalty
  • Each repeat of a genre costs a little
  • Apply my "more like this" and "hide" feedback

Optional: a language model re-ranks the top 60

Only if I add a Gemini key. Its ranking counts for 65% and the score for 35%, so a wrong answer cannot turn the feed upside down. The language and variety rules still run after it.

Cached until the library changes

One feed costs over 40 requests to the film database. It is kept for 3 hours, and any log, rating or dismissal clears it.

With fewer than three logged titles there is no history yet, so the feed ranks well-rated titles in my languages instead.
