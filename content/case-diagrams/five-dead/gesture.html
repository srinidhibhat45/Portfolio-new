How a card leaves your hand

Small movements stay in the hand. A clear toss up plays.

0 pxFinger down on a card
5 pxThe card lifts and follows your grip
12 pxA quick flick up plays
16–24 pxA broad toss up plays, at any speed
TableOr drop it anywhere on the table
One tap→Selects the card. Play commits it.
Double-tap or double-click→Plays it.
Toss up, or drop on the table→Plays it. No aiming needed.
Drag back into the hand→Cancels. The card returns to its slot.
Flat swipe on a long hand→Scrolls the hand. Nothing is played.
Unsafe or rejected card→Flies back to where it came from.

Distances are how far the finger moves upward. Keyboard users keep the select-then-Play controls. Not drawn to scale.
