No safe card? You lose one of your five lives, keep your hand, and the total goes back to 0.

Empty your hand? Every other player loses a life. In the standard game, the last player with lives left wins.

Each player starts with ten cards and five lives. The rules follow the published leaflet for 5 Alive.
