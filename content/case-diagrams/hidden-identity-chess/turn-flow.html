The rules in one picture

On your turn you can do one of three things

You see your own army. Every enemy piece is a shield until it is captured.

Your turn

You know what your own pieces are. You know where the enemy pieces are, but not what they are.

→

Move

A normal chess move. Only legal squares are offered.

→

Capture a piece and it is revealed. Capture the king and you win. There is no check or checkmate.

Bluff

Move your piece to any empty or enemy square, legal or not.

→

The game keeps a private record of whether it was legal. Your opponent sees only where the piece went.

Challenge

Accuse any earlier enemy move that has not been checked yet.

→

It was legal. Nothing is revealed and nobody loses a piece.

It was illegal. That piece is removed, and you pick one more enemy piece to remove (two if it was already captured). A second proven offence loses the game.

From the game's written rules. A challenge and its penalty do not use up your move. A king capture waits for the other player to review it, so an illegal one can still be caught.
