Personas · a design aid, not interview findings

Two readers of the same panel

Rutik and Sneha are not real people. I wrote them to picture an expert and a beginner. They are not backed by interviews.

The expert

Rutik, 29

SEO engineer in Pune. Three years in SEO, and a QA engineer before that.

“I want an SEO application that can make my life easier at work. Nothing fancy, simple and easy to use.”

Goals

  • To be proficient in SEO analysis and research
  • To have the best SEO-optimised products for his company

Frustration

Current SEO apps are unable to help him with his work.

What matters to him

ComfortConveniencePrice
The beginner

Sneha, 31

Content marketer and small business owner in Kolkata. She wants to take her business global.

“I want to be in line with trends, especially in SEO, so I can keep my website updated at all times. SEO means leads and leads mean business!”

Goals

  • To grow her business and get leads
  • To get her website to the top of the search page

Frustration

She is not technically inclined, so normal SEO tools are a hassle to understand.

What matters to her

ComfortConveniencePrice
