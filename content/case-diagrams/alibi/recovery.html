Getting ready for a real group

What happens when something goes wrong mid-game

A clip will not play
→
The player can retry, or tap Clip won't play? Tell the host. The host presses Replace clips: everyone gets fresh clips in the same round, and no scores are lost.
A connection drops
→
The seat and anything already submitted are kept for 90 seconds. Refreshing the same tab puts the player back.
Someone leaves mid-round
→
The round goes back to the lobby, so nobody plays a round that is missing a player.
The host leaves
→
Control passes to another real player. Practice players are never made host.
One player stalls
→
Each phase has a timer, so one slow player cannot hold up the room. A missing clue shows as a dash.
A friend arrives late
→
They join in the lobby or after a reveal, never in the middle of a round. A room holds up to eight.
