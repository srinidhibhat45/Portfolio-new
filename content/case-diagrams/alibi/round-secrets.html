The room has to share

  • Which phase the round is in, and how long is left
  • Who has finished watching, locked a clue or voted, as a tick
  • Every clue, at the same moment, once all are in
  • The result, both clips and every vote, at the reveal

Each player has to keep

  • Which clip they were sent. Nobody is told their role, not even Player 3
  • Their own clue, until everyone has locked one in
  • Their own vote, until the reveal
  • Who the imposter is, until the reveal
