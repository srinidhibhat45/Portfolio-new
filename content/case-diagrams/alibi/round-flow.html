How a round runs

Six phases, each with its own clock

Lobby

no clock

3 to 8 players. The host picks a clip pack, rounds, clip length and talk time.

Moves on when everyone is ready and the host starts

→

Watch

15, 20 or 30 s

Each player plays their own clip. The in-app call is muted. 30 extra seconds allow for slow loading.

Moves on when all have watched, or time runs out

→

One word

60 s

One word, up to 24 characters. Others see only a tick that you are done.

Moves on when all clues are in. A missing clue shows as a dash

→

Discuss

60 to 180 s

All clues appear together. The group talks it through.

Moves on when time runs out, or the host opens voting early

→

Vote

45 s

One vote each, for someone else. It cannot be changed.

Moves on when all have voted, or time runs out

→

Reveal

no clock

Both clips, every vote, the scores. +1 for each correct vote. +2 if the imposter escapes. A tie lets them escape.

The host starts the next round

Shaded phases hold something private. Times are the game's own settings and limits.
