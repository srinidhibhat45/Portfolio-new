In the browser

One source module per feed

Each fetches the feed through a shared cache and turns it into one shape. When a feed fails, the app carries on without it.

Worked out locally

Sun, moon, planets, twilight, the night side, station passes.

Bundled catalogues

22 solar eclipses, meteor showers, 48 landmark events.

What you see

The globe

MapLibre GL JS 5 in globe mode. It unrolls into a flat map as you zoom in.

The readout panel

Place, Weather, Sky and The Log, plus a record view for any event.

Status line

Live counts and the name of every source.

No framework, no build step, no accounts and no API keys. Hosted as plain files on Netlify.
