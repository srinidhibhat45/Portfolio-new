What went wrong, and the fix in version 1.17

Adverts were being saved as income

Before: accept anything a block list misses

Get cover of Rs.50,00,000 for your family. Pay the premium with your credit card.

1The word "credit" matched the rule for money coming in.
2The only number in it was read as the amount.
3No keyword on the block list matched this wording, so it was saved.

+ ₹50,00,000 income, from an advert

After: ask for proof that money moved

Get cover of Rs.50,00,000 for your family. Pay the premium with your ###########.

1Card names are blanked out before direction is read. "Credit card" is a product, not a payment.
2A number framed as an offer ("cover of", "up to", "get") is not an amount.
3Nothing says money moved, and there are no account digits, reference, balance or time. A real alert carries several of these.

Not saved

The insurance example is the one recorded in the project notes. The message wording here is made up to match it. ₹50,00,000 is ₹50 lakh.
