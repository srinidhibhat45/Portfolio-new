The problem in one picture · made-up messages

Bank senders send far more than payments

Six messages that could arrive from the same bank in one day. Only one of them moved money.

Bank alert

Rs.240.00 debited from A/c XX0000 to KETTLE HOUSE CAFE. UPI Ref 600000000001. Avl Bal Rs.18,560.00.

EntryA real payment. Amount, merchant, account and reference are all there.

One-time password

OTP ●●●●●● for a payment of Rs.999 at an online store. Do not share it.

SkipIt quotes an amount, but nothing has been paid yet.

Card advert

Your new credit card is ready! Get up to Rs.50,000 credit limit. Apply now.

SkipIt says "credit" twice and quotes a big number. Neither means money arrived.

Card statement

Statement for card XX0000: total due Rs.6,210.00, minimum due Rs.310.00, due by 05-10-26.

SkipTotals for a month, not a movement. The due date is not a future payment.

Failed payment

Your payment of Rs.1,200.00 to NORTHLINE CABS has failed. Any amount debited will be refunded.

SkipThe word "debited" is there, but no money moved.

Payment request

LEAF AND LOAF has requested Rs.180.00 from you. Approve in your UPI app.

SkipA request, not a payment. It may never be approved.

Every message here is made up. The verdicts are the parser's own rejection reasons: OTP, advert, statement, failed, future or requested payment.
