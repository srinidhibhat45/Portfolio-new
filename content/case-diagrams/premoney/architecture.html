How the app is put together

Screens never touch the database, and one token refreshes them all

Screens
HomeActivity and reviewInsightsGoals and budgetsScoresSettings and toolsOnboarding

↕

State

Riverpod providers. After any write, one "data version" number goes up, and every view that depends on data reloads.

↕

Domain logic
SMS parserDedupe keyTemplate skeletonCategoriserScoresRecurring chargesCSV exportSubscriptions

↕

Repositories

One per table, with hand-written SQL. The only place SQL is allowed.

↕

SQLite

One local file. A unique index on the message key makes a second copy impossible.

Background capture

Runs on its own when the app is closed, with its own database connection and no shared state. The app refreshes when it comes back to the front.

Android side

The SMS receiver is declared in the app's manifest. Updates are handed to Android's installer.

Optional services

Firebase for sign-in and backup. Groq for AI suggestions. Both are off until used.

Flutter and Dart, Android only. iOS does not let apps read text messages, which is the core input.
