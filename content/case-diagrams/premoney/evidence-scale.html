Check 5, the proof test

The more a message reads like an advert, the more proof it needs

Marks a bank leaves on a real alert

Masked account or card digits (A/c XX0000)+2
A settlement reference number+2
The balance or limit left afterwards+2
An anti-fraud line ("Not you?")+2
A time of day+1
A date+1
A named channel (UPI, card, ATM, transfer)+1
A past-tense verb right beside the amount+1

Proof needed, by number of advert tells

No advert wording2 marks
1 or 2 tells4 marks
3 or more tells6 marks

Tells are phrases like "up to", "get Rs", "click", "valid till". A web link counts as two.

Points and thresholds are the parser's own. The two examples are made up and scored by hand from those rules, as an illustration. A message I have marked "always import" skips this check.
