How "messages like this" is decided

Each message is reduced to the shape of its template

Two sends of one made-up advert

Spent Rs.2,999 on your card? Convert it to EMI. Offer ref AB1234 valid till 30-09-26.

Spent Rs.7,450 on your card? Convert it to EMI. Offer ref CD5678 valid till 15-10-26.

→

The skeleton both reduce to

spent rs.# on your card? convert it to emi. offer ref # valid till #.

Amounts, dates, references and masked digits are blanked. What is left is the wording the sender reuses.

→

Is a new message the same template?

Same skeleton

A match.

82% or more of the words shared

Also a match. Set high on purpose: a false match would silently drop a real payment.

A different merchant

Always a different template, so blocking one shop's advert never blocks payments to another.

The messages are made up. The blanking, the 0.82 overlap threshold and the merchant rule are taken from the app's code and notes.
