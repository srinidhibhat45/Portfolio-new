How a photo becomes a score, traced through the code

Two copies of each photo, three layers of scoring

On the phone

Photo taken

Full quality, with the camera's own data: make, model, aperture, shutter speed.

→

Made at the same time

Scoring copy

64 × 64 pixels, saved as PNG so no compression shifts the colour. Never uploaded.

Upload copy

WebP at quality 0.82, longest side 1,080 pixels. Sent only when you share.

Place and camera data

Location if you allow it, plus the camera fields above.

→

Three layers, multiplied

AColour match

Each of the 4,096 pixels is compared with the target in Lab, a colour space built around how people see difference. Pixels close enough count. More of them, and closer ones, score higher, with diminishing returns.

BComposition

Variety of colours (55%) and edges (45%). A flat wall is cut to 18%; low detail to 70%.

CReal-photo guess

Smoothness and missing camera data. It can take off at most 22%.

→
