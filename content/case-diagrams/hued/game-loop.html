The game loop, traced through the code

One day in Hued, from new colour to standings

1A new colour

At midnight UTC every phone works out the same colour, its name and a prompt.

2Hunt and shoot

The camera opens with the colour pinned on top. Live photos only.

3Scored on the phone

A score from 0 to 100, a verdict and three checks. Nothing is uploaded yet.

4Share and lock

The photo uploads. The database allows one post per person per day.

5Streak

A database rule adds a day, or starts again at 1 after a gap.

6Standings and feed

Ranked among friends or in a group, today or yesterday. Votes and comments in the feed; the post can also appear in your group chats.

Then tomorrow

A new colour, and step 1 again. The standings keep yesterday's ranking one tap away.

Already posted today?

The share button becomes Save to draft. The app says the best draft is submitted at the end of the day, but in this build drafts are only held in the phone's memory. That step is not built yet.
