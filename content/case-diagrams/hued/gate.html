The checks before a photo can be shared, in the order the code runs them

Hard stops for cheats, soft notes for weak photos

1. Is it a flat wall of colour?

Very little colour variety and almost no edges.

yes→
Blocked. "Find the colour inside a real scene."
↓ NO

2. Is the camera data missing?

It needs a camera make or model and at least one exposure detail, such as aperture or shutter speed.

yes→
Blocked. It looks like a screenshot or a photo of a photo.
↓ NO

3. Real-photo guess above 85%?

A last stop for very high suspicion.

yes→
Blocked. It looks generated or heavily edited.
↓ NO
Can be shared. Low detail lowers the score and turns the Detail check into a warning. Less than 2% of the frame in the colour turns the Colour check into a warning and adds a tip.

What the real-photo guess can and cannot do

It mixes two signals: how smooth the image is, and whether camera data is missing. Missing data already stops a photo at step 2.

So for a photo that passes step 2, the guess is at most 55%. It can lower the score by about 12% at most, and it can never block it.

It is a tentative heuristic, not a reliable detector of AI images.
