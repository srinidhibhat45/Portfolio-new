How the daily colour changed, from the project history

Four versions of the daily colour in eleven days

25 June

A list of 48 colours

Hand-picked, each with a name and a one-line prompt. The date chose one.

→
26 June, 10:44

A list of 137

Enough for about four and a half months before a colour came back.

→
26 June, 10:54

Made from the date

No list. The date is turned into a number, and the number into a hue, a saturation between 35% and 90% and a lightness between 25% and 72%. Never grey, never neon, never black or white.

→
5 July

Weighted toward colours you can find

Six candidates a day. Each gets a findability score, and the draw favours the findable ones. Names and prompts come from 16 hue families.

Dates are from the commit history. The swatches in the first two cards are the actual lists from the code at those dates.
