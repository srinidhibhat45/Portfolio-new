The visual rules

Every box says what kind of thing it is

Each part of the library takes one role. The role sets its line, fill and corner, so a wireframe reads at a glance.

Structure

Faint dashed line. Disappears when you squint.

Surface

Solid line on paper, rounded. A card you can see.

Raised

A hard shadow. Floats above the page.

Field

Thin grey line, tinted well, nearly square. "Type here."

Control

Thick ink, rounded. "Click here."

Bar

A tinted strip of chrome, square.

Bare

Text and icons, no box at all.

Media

A 45° hatch. Still reads as "picture" at thumbnail size.

Sticky note

Paper with a cut corner, never a rectangle.

Annotation

A second, red ink. Notes about the design, never part of it.

Redrawn from the product's own rules. The real parts also wobble, through the sketch filter.
