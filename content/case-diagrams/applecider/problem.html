Three ways to show an idea in its first hour

Paper sketch

  • +Fast, and nobody mistakes it for finished
  • –Cannot move a block or share the file
  • –The header is redrawn on every screen

AppleCider

  • +Still looks like a sketch
  • +Real canvas: move, reuse, undo, link
  • +Saved as a file you keep

Polished mock-up

  • +Movable, shareable, reusable
  • –Looks decided before it is
  • –Invites debate about colour and corners

Drawn from the reasoning in the project's own notes. An illustration of the idea, not a measured comparison.
