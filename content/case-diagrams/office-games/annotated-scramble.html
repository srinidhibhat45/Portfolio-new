1The room's leaderboard. A green box means that player has already finished the word.
2Each round has a time limit, 30 seconds by default.
3Found letters turn green. The large orange letter is the key just pressed. Here it was wrong, so the word shakes and a mistake is counted.
4A hint. As time runs down, a few keys at a time flip to show what they really type.
5The keyboard is the same for everyone in the room, and new every round.
