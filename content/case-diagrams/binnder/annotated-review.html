1Filters by kind: all files, images, videos, audio, PDFs and other.
2The queue is sorted largest first, and says how many files are left.
3Opens the file full screen. Tapping the preview does the same.
4The preview takes all the height that is left. Here it is page one of a sample PDF.
5Name, full location and size. Details opens a sheet with the full path and date.
6Keep, Delete forever and Bin sit below the card, so they never scroll away. Delete forever is red and has no fill.
