Five seconds to undo. Then it waits in the bin for 30 days and can be restored.

A label with the action appears after about a third of the distance, so you see the choice before you make it.

The choice counts at 96 dp, roughly 15 mm of travel. Up only counts when the drag is clearly more up than sideways.

Each swipe has a matching button below the card, and a named action for screen readers.
