The board

Frames, columns, connectors, pins

1

A card opts in

Only cards in a day frame or wired to another card get a time. A loose card stays exactly as it is.

→
AfterCricket match?Lunch 12:30
2

It only pushes forward

A card on the wrong day changes date and keeps its hour. Nothing is pulled earlier, and no time is made up.

BeforeFri 18:00 dinnersitting in Sat frame
→
AfterSat 18:00 dinner
3

Reordering swaps times

Drag a card above another and the column's times are dealt back out in the new order, lengths kept.

BeforeLunch 1 hSession 3 h
→
AfterSession 3 hLunch 1 h, after it
4

Pins win

A pinned card never moves. If something runs into it, you are told instead of quietly overruled.

BeforeTransfer runs longPinned flight
→
AfterTransfer, flaggedPinned flight

Rules as built. The before and after cards are drawn to explain each rule, not taken from the app. On a freshly tidied board, Resolve changes nothing, and a test checks that.
