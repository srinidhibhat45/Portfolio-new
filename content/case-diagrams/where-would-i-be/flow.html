How it works

Three steps, one at a time

Steps 1 and 2 use the same dock at the bottom of the screen. The place picker turns into the time dial.

On screen first

1Pick a place

  • Use my location
  • Search a city or town
  • Tap the globe anywhere
  • Surprise me
→

Then

2Run the clock

  • One sentence: where the ground ends up, how far, which way
  • A slider from 500 million years ago to 500 million years ahead
  • Five jumps: 1 kyr, 1 Myr, 10 Myr, 50 Myr, 250 Myr
  • Play runs the journey in about seven seconds
→

Only when asked

3Open the details

  • Coordinates and the change in latitude
  • Distance, direction, climate band
  • Who ends up next door
  • The plate, its neighbours, and how far to trust the answer

Or compare two places

When do they come closest, and how close?

Kyr is a thousand years. Myr is a million years.
