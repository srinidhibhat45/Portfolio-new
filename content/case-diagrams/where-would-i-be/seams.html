One decision, four tries

How the plate boundaries were shown, version by version

First version

Coloured from the start

Seams coloured by type, with the grid and the orbit circle also on. The colours were explained only in a popover behind an icon.

Default: everything on

Rebuild 1

A key that never leaves

A permanent key: pulling apart, crashing together, sliding past. I called the missing key the biggest comprehension failure. Grid, borders and orbit went off.

Default: colours and key on

Rebuild 2

Nothing until asked

Six line styles at once was too much, so the seams went off with everything else. Switching the colours on brought the key with them.

Default: seams hidden

Now

A grey hairline, always

Hiding the seams was wrong: they are the reason the answer is what it is. A faint line shows where they are. Colour and key come together, on request.

Default: hairline on, colour off

All four steps happened between 19 and 20 September 2026, in the product's commit history.
