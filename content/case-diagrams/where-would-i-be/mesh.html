The hardest build problem

Two bugs in how the continents were cut into triangles

1. Seas painted as land

Afro-Eurasia is one outline of 2,472 points. Its long thin triangles were split at their 3D midpoints, which bent them tens of degrees off course and over the Kara and Laptev Seas.

Fix: split in the flat longitude and latitude plane they were cut in.

290 → 1of 22,452 sea test points drawn as land

2. Gaps near the poles

Near a pole an edge can cross half the globe in longitude and still look short. A test on length left triangles too coarse, and dark gaps showed through.

Fix: split an edge when it sags too far from where it should run.
