1The ring being edited. Everywhere is the main ring. Each app ring is listed beside it.
2How many slots are filled. A ring can have 4 to 12.
3The selected slot gets a blue outline and its name, in the same place it has in the real ring.
4The tab marks a folder. Double-click to edit the actions inside.
5An empty slot: a dashed circle with a plus. One click opens the picker.
6Replace the action or clear the slot.
7What the slot does. For an app: open it, show or hide it, or quit it.
8How the slot looks: its label, icon and tint colour.

Rendered by the Mac app. Slots can also be dragged onto each other to swap them, and every change can be undone.
