The problem in one picture

Muting a call while you write in another app

Without a ring

1
Find the meeting window

Switch apps with ⌘-Tab or the Dock, and look for it.

2
Press its shortcut

Teams mutes with ⇧⌘M, but only while Teams is in front.

3
Find your way back

Switch again and find where you were typing.

Three trips, and one more shortcut to remember for every app.

With Halo

1
Press one shortcut, or a mouse button

The ring opens around the pointer, wherever it is.

2
Flick toward Mute and let go

Halo brings Teams forward, presses its shortcut, and returns you to your document.

The same shortcut opens every action. The pointer does not leave your work.

An illustration of the idea, not a timed comparison. The Teams shortcut is the one Microsoft documents for the Mac app.
