How a choice is made

One shortcut, two ways to choose, told apart by time

Press the shortcut

The ring opens around the pointer. Then it depends on how you let go.

→

The line between a tap and a hold is 0.28 s by default. It can be set anywhere from 0.15 s to 0.8 s.

Let go quickly, nothing highlighted

The ring stays open

Click a slot, press its number, or use the arrows and Return.

Move toward a slot, then let go

That slot runs

Even a fast flick counts. The pointer can travel past the ring.

Hold past 0.28 s, nothing highlighted

The ring closes

Nothing runs. This is how you back out.

Let go over an empty slot

Nothing runs

Clicking an empty slot shakes it instead.

Three modes in Settings

Automatic (the default)

Both ways, as above.

Tap, then click

Letting go never runs anything. For mouse software that sends only a quick tap.

Hold and release

Letting go runs the highlight or closes the ring.

Drawn from the selection rules in the spec. Each of these outcomes has its own unit test on both platforms.
