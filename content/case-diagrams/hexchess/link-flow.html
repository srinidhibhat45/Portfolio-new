Playing by link

The whole game travels in the link, one move at a time

You

Make a move

Then tap Share game link. You can queue one premove while you wait.

The link

Carries every move

After the # sign, which a browser does not send to the server.

Your friend

Opens it and plays one move

As the colour assigned to them, with the board turned to their side.

The reply

A new link back

Your friend shares the updated game. Always use the newest one.

You

Open the reply

Every move is replayed and checked. A queued premove is checked again, then played.

Turned away, board unchanged

  • Your own outgoing link, opened again
  • A reply that changes moves already saved on this device
  • A reply that adds more than one move, or an illegal one

What a link cannot do

  • Prove who sent it. There are no accounts
  • Stop someone reading it, or using a chess engine on the side
  • Stay short in a long game. A game file can be exported instead

The share dialog says this too: friendly play, with no way to verify who sent a link or to stop outside engine help.
