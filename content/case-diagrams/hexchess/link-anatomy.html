moves

Only the moves, never the board. The receiving app replays them through the rules, so a made-up position cannot be slipped in.

id and sender

Which game this is and who sent it. That is how the app knows an old link, or your own, when it sees one.

drawOffer and result

A draw needs an offer in one link and an acceptance in the reply.

The size

Encoded as base64 text. The app refuses anything over 90,000 characters.

Decoded from a real link after one move, made on a local copy of the app. The domain is shown as it would be on the live site. Some fields are left out.
