The build

The data check reads the whole graph before anything ships

Errors the build stops

Broken references

  • A parent or spouse link points at nobody
  • A gotra, matha, deity or home village the lists do not know
  • Father and mother are the same person, or someone is their own parent
  • A temple photograph that has lost its photographer or licence

Impossible dates and loops

  • Died before being born, or born in the future
  • A parent under 12 at the birth, or a child born after the mother died
  • Married under the age of 10, or after dying
  • Someone who ends up as their own ancestor

Warnings reported only

  • A life longer than 115 years
  • A parent over 65 at the birth
  • Born more than a year after the father died
  • Marked living, with a death year
  • Names people proposed that no maintainer has confirmed yet

Warnings do not block anything. Real families produce records that look wrong and are not.

None of these show when you read one record. All of them show when you check the graph. The list is from the check's own code.
