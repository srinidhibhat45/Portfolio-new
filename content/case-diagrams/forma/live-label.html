Two clocks, two labels

"Last read" is when Forma last checked. "Latest reported record" is when the reading was taken. A fresh check never makes an old reading look new.

Older readings keep their date

Weight or blood pressure may be days old. When the latest value is carried forward, its date goes with it, and charts keep the gaps.

The bars are not to scale. The 30-second and 5-minute limits are the app's own.
