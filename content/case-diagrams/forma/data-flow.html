How it works

Forma reads what Samsung Health shares, and nothing else

The main path: history and scores

Galaxy watch

Measures steps, heart rate, sleep and more. Syncs to the phone through Samsung's own app.

→

Samsung Health

Decides which records it publishes to Health Connect, and when.

→

Health Connect

Android's shared store for health records. The person grants Forma each type of record, one by one. Some or none is fine.

→

Forma on the phone

Reads only Samsung Health's records. Totals come from Android's own adding-up, so the same steps are not added twice. Saved in private storage.

Forma on the watch

Optional. A 60-second heart check, and batched daily activity.

→

Wear OS link

Encrypted. Can pass through Google's relay, which the setup screen says.

→

A separate short-lived store

Heart rate is called "live" for 30 seconds at most. Watch steps can lead the step ring for 5 minutes. These readings never enter history, exports or scores.

No account, no Forma server, no advertising and no analytics. Updates come from signed releases on GitHub.
