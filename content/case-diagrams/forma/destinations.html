The structure

Five places in the bottom bar, four rows in settings

The bottom bar

Today

Activity rings and targets, then sleep, recovery and energy, then heart rate with its time.

Sleep

Last night by stage, then seven nights of length, bedtime and wake time.

Move

Hour-by-hour activity, the week, step streaks, and workouts over 7, 28, 90 or 365 days.

Trends

Pick a measurement and compare 7, 28 or 90 days. Tap a point to read it.

Health

44 measurements in six groups: heart, vitals, body, movement, sleep and food. Each one has a history.

Settings

Devices

Is the watch connected, and the one next step.

Targets

Sleep, steps, calories, exercise and water.

Your data

How many days and measurements, export, import, delete.

App updates

Check, download and install new versions.

Personal tools

Optional: a journal, manual workouts, a water log and a two-minute breathing exercise. None of them leads a main page.
