Auto-animate

It looks at a slide the way a designer would before moving anything

1

What kind of slide is this?

Cover, section break, list, a row of cards, picture and copy, chart, a page too dense to stage, closing.

2

What sits behind what?

Backdrop, then structure, then media, then words, then accents. Nothing arrives in front of the thing it sits on.

3

What repeats?

Three cards in a row arrive card by card, not line by line in a diagonal sweep.

4

What is the subject?

The main image or the title gets a beat to itself. A caption never lands before its photo.

5

How much is there?

Past about twenty layers, one at a time stops helping, so a dense page arrives in three soft waves.
