Persona · written from the interviews

Vivek, 20, third-year engineering student in Goa

Vivek is not a real person. He stands for the students I spoke to.

His situation

Studious, and regular in class. When teaching moved online, each of his teachers picked a different app. His study material is now spread across three or four of them, and it is hard to get to when he needs it.

What he wants

  • Classes back in person soon
  • One consistent way for the college to communicate
  • One app that does everything
  • Resources he can reach when the time comes
  • To do well in his studies

What gets in the way

  • Online classes are stressful
  • His phone is not high-end and lacks the storage for every app teachers ask for
  • The stress and anxiety keep building
  • New apps take time to figure out, and it is embarrassing to ask
