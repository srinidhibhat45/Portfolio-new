## Final prompts

### Stationery

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: a finished wide landscape service-card image for an Indian design engineer's portfolio. Primary request: a beautifully art-directed, photorealistic stationery and packaging mockup, not an icon. Scene: warm pale limestone studio surface. Subject: a slim deep burgundy presentation booklet resting on a slightly offset ivory box, two thick ivory business cards in front, one tiny subtle gold geometric Indian jaali emboss on the booklet. Composition: 3:2 horizontal image, objects entirely visible centered with generous breathing room, camera at a refined three-quarter angle, no clutter. Lighting: soft natural light from upper left, realistic delicate contact shadows. Palette: wine #532d3a, warm ivory, restrained antique brass. Materials: real uncoated paper grain, matte burgundy cover, fine foil emboss. Constraints: no people, no mock browser icons, no line art, no gradients, no text or lettering, no watermarks, no overdecorated border, no flowers, no fake brand. This should look like a premium independent design studio photograph with clean craftsmanship.

### Portrait

Use case: style-transfer. Asset type: a finished 3:2 landscape service-card portrait illustration. Input image: the attached local portrait is the identity reference. Primary request: create a sophisticated low-poly geometric illustration of this exact smiling Indian man. Preserve his face shape, short dark hair, beard, warm smile and white collared shirt, clearly recognizable. Medium: carefully composed solid-color triangular polygon facets, mature editorial illustration, rich natural skin tones with warm shadows, graphite-black hair, ivory shirt, restrained burgundy shadows. No outlines, no wireframe, no 3D plastic, no cartoon emoji, no texture noise, no exaggerated features. Composition: bust portrait centered, full head including hair entirely inside frame, face prominent and shoulders visible, calm flat muted ochre/cream background, generous blank space left and right, wide landscape 3:2 image. Lighting modeled through facets, polished coherent planes with just enough complexity to convey identity. No lettering, no icons, no borders, no watermarks.
