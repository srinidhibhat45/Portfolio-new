# Indian textile direction — 9 October 2026

The decorative system uses original SVG drawings, with the mix of solid space, asymmetric panels and varied motif scales informed by the user's clothing references:

- [MyDesignation, Pride of India](https://www.mydesignation.com/collections/pride-of-india), including the Karigar shirt's geometric borders and folk-print panels.
- [The Souled Store, printed shirts](https://api.thesouledstore.com/tags/printed-shirts).

These are contemporary interpretations inspired by textile forms, not reproductions of brand artwork or claims of authentic traditional craft.

## Composition

- Portrait: one geometric radial medallion, slowly rotating behind the photograph.
- Hero seams and built products: feathered ikat-like chevrons.
- Design work: an Ajrakh-inspired eight-point block print, with a stitched header ribbon.
- About and contact: finely engraved paisley/pallu forms, cropped at the lower left.
- Timeline, gallery and visitor board: Kutch-inspired stepped diamonds and running stitches, placed as hems and narrow side seams.
- Services and testimonials: cusped arches and architectural jaali.
- Brain: a Bandhani-inspired tied-dot hem, kept away from the interactive connections.
- Writing room: an original pen-drawn tree, buds, leaves and birds beside the notebook.
- Mobile menu: a paisley pallu and a narrow woven seam that does not sit under the links.

Wine, dusty indigo, terracotta, sage and antique gold are restrained by section-specific opacity and fading masks. All decorations are non-interactive and hidden from assistive technology.

## Motion and verification

Reduced-motion preferences disable movement; static audit mode pauses the new panel and portrait animation. The existing artwork marquee and its pause/grid controls are preserved.
